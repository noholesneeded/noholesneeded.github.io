About No Holes Needed

No Holes Needed is a problem-solving blog for people living in rented or small homes. The constraint is the point: no drilling if the lease forbids it, no renovations you can't take with you, and no advice that treats a deposit as expendable.

Every guide starts from a real complaint renters keep repeating — a landlord who won't share the paint color, a studio with almost no closet, thin walls, a dark living room, a bedroom that runs hot, a litter box in a one-bedroom, humidity on the windows, a work-from-home setup in a room that is also the bedroom.

It is not a generic "cozy apartment" inspiration site. If a post doesn't fix a specific, recurring problem, it doesn't belong here.

What you'll find here

  • Problem-first guides. Hanging art and curtains without holes, storage with no closet, noise, light, heat and humidity, sleep, pets, and small-space work setups.
  • Honest limits. We describe what usually fails, when adhesive is a bad bet, and whether a product is likely to come off cleanly at move-out. We do not invent reviews, star ratings, test results, or personal "we tried this for three years" stories.
  • Products you can take with you. Recommendations are lease-safe and reversible whenever we can make them so. Always confirm the current listing, weight rating, and return policy before you buy.

Who's behind it
