Last updated November 12, 2026.

This policy explains what happens to information when you visit noholesneeded.github.io ("the site"). If a custom domain is added later, this policy will be updated to name that domain.

We don't ask you to create an account, and the site has no comment sections. We load Pinterest's official Save button script, described below. Google Analytics and, when advertising is approved, Google AdSense may use cookies as described in the sections below.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may collect basic technical information, such as your IP address, for security and to deliver the site. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Fonts

Our fonts are hosted on this site, so loading a page doesn't send your information to a third-party font service.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-PMR2JYW0LT) to understand how the site is used, such as which pages are read, roughly where visitors are, and what kind of browser or device they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect identifiers such as IP addresses. This information is processed by Google. You can read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Search

Site search runs in your browser. It uses a list of our guides that we host on this site, and it does not send your search to a third-party search company. If you submit a search, the words may appear in the page address and in our host's ordinary server logs.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads on this site through Google AdSense, using publisher ID pub-1406584416220176. When those ads are present, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

To learn more about how Google uses data when you use our site, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
